Our story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood place for handmade baked goods. Our goal is to make everyday visits feel welcoming and help customers find food to enjoy with the people they care about.

Our sourcing philosophy

We value thoughtful ingredient choices and clear conversations about what goes into our food. Ask about ingredients before ordering, especially if you have an allergy or dietary concern.
